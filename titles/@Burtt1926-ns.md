> [!abstract]
> To all businessmen seriously concerned with the vital question of personnel selection, Professor Burtt’s book offers invaluable suggestions for the practical solution of their problems. It has all the lucidity of a textbook, and the avoidance—wherever possible—of all technical terms allows the lay reader to grasp easily the immense possibilities opened by this new field of psychology, and to apply intelligently its latest methods and discoveries to his own everyday business requirements. The student and expert in scientific management will find it the most up-to-date and complete book available on the subject, and the only one that will give him a substantial foundation upon which to build his own training for personnel work.
