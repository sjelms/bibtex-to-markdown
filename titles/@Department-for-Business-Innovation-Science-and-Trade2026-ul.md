> [!abstract]
> The Construction Jobs Plan establishes a national framework to address workforce and skills challenges in the UK construction sector. It sets out how government, industry, local leaders and delivery partners will collaborate to increase workforce capacity, improve recruitment, training, retention and progression, and overcome barriers to investment in skills development. The plan supports government ambitions to deliver 1.5 million homes, invest £725 billion in infrastructure over ten years, and upgrade 5 million existing buildings. It identifies challenges arising from the industry's fragmented structure, high levels of self-employment, predominance of small and micro businesses, uncertain project pipelines and limited training capacity. The plan outlines ten industry commitments, including strategic workforce planning, sector skills plans, competence frameworks, improved employment practices, apprenticeship support and the adoption of artificial intelligence and industrialised construction. Government support includes a £625 million Construction Skills Package to expand training provision, strengthen further education and apprenticeship pathways, and increase entry into the sector. The plan also considers regional and devolved skills needs and establishes a framework for coordinating government and industry actions to meet future construction workforce demand.
