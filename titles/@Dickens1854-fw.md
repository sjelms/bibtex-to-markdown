> Dickens, Charles. 1854. "\textit Hard Times: For These Times." [[Bradbury and Evans]].

> [!abstract]
> Hard Times is a critique of industrialization, utilitarianism, and rigid fact-based education set in the fictional northern English mill town of Coketown. It follows educator Thomas Gradgrind, wealthy factory owner Josiah Bounderby, and honest mill worker Stephen Blackpool.
