> [!abstract]
> The construction industry continues to face significant challenges in ensuring the health and safety (H&S) of its workers. Traditional training methods are often insufficient in preparing workers for the complexities of real-world scenarios. This paper explores the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Virtual Reality (VR) technologies in H&S training within the construction sector. By using AI to personalise and adapt VR training experiences, these technologies can significantly enhance learning outcomes, improve safety behaviour, and ultimately reduce workplace accidents.
