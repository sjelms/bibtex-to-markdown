> [!abstract]
> When policy-makers attempt to devise an industrial strategy, there is a tendency to adopt a silo approach. Hence, we get separate sections on productivity, innovation, research capacity, infrastructure, skills and so on. These silos become populated with their own stakeholders and vested interests, and they lack incentives to collaborate. In this chapter, we argue for a more holistic, co-production approach with strategies for the monitoring, development and utilization of skills and capacity at its heart to reflect the interconnected and dynamic reality of economic activity at all levels from the local to the global.
