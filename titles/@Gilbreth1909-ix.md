> [!abstract]
> Frank Gilbreth's book "Brick Laying System" has been a classic in its field but long out of print since its first publication in 1909. The Gilbreth Bricklaying System is a pioneering time-and-motion study framework developed by industrial engineer Frank Bunker Gilbreth Sr. in 1909, which drastically reduced wasted physical effort and increased masonry productivity by nearly 200%. Frank Gilbreth began his career as a bricklayer's apprentice in 1885 and noticed that every worker used different methods—some fast, some slow, and some used for teaching. By breaking the task down into basic human motions, he eliminated unnecessary steps and designed a systematic environment for the mason.
