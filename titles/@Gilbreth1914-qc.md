> [!abstract]
> The book explores the importance of individuality, measurement, standardization, teaching, incentives, and welfare in enhancing efficiency and productivity in the workplace. It contrasts traditional and scientific management approaches, emphasizing the scientific study of workers and the integration of welfare into the system. Through practical examples and psychological insights, the book provides a comprehensive guide to implementing scientific management principles for optimal performance and worker satisfaction.
