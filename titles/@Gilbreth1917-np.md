> [!bibliography]
> Gilbreth, Frank Bunker, Lillian Moller Gilbreth. 1917. "Applied Motion Study: A Collection of Papers on the Efficient Method to Industrial Preparedness." [[Sturgis and Walton Company]].

> [!abstract]
> A foundational text in industrial engineering that outlines how eliminating wasted physical movement can maximize productivity, reduce fatigue, and prepare national industries for efficiency. This book aims to describe Motion Study as applied to various fields of activity and in such a way as to make possible its application in any and all kinds of work. Motion Study is a means to permanent and practical waste elimination hence a prerequisite to efficient preparedness that shall be adequate, constructive and cumulative.
