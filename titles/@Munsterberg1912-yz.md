> [!bibliography]
> Münsterberg, Hugo. 1912. "Psychology and Economic Life: A Contribution to Applied Experimental Psychology." [[J A Barth]].

> [!abstract]
> This edition examines how psychological research applies to modern economies, exploring how workers, managers, and organizations interact in real-world settings. The text frames a practical program of study, presenting experimental approaches to understanding workplace behavior, productivity, and the impacts of management decisions. It combines theory with concrete observations from laboratory and field settings, offering insights into how psychological principles shape efficiency, motivation, and organizational culture.
