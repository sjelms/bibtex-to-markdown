> [!abstract]
> It is an honour to address the Conference on the Acquisition of Symbolic Skills, and also a pleasure. The topic of this conference is an important one, about which we all still have much to learn. It is important because the concept of symbolic skill lies quite close - closer than might have been expected - to our conception of human nature itself. Animals don’t exhibit symbolic skills, and neither do computers - so I shall argue - but people do. Sometimes even psychologists do. My aim here is to clarify these ideas as much as possible - to say what skills are, and what symbolic skills might be, and why they are interesting. You must not expect, however, that my attempt at clarification will be entirely successful. I am not quite skillful enough for that.
