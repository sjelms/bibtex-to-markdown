> Oliver, Damian, Serena Yu, John Buchanan. 2019. "Political economy of vocational education and training." [[John Wiley and Sons Inc]]. http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/9781119098713.ch7

> [!abstract]
> This chapter reviews various political economy approaches to understand the role of vocational education and training (VET) in society and the economy. Human capital theory (HCT) draws on neoclassical economics to argue that VET is best understood by focusing on the individual's decision to invest in training in order to increase their stock of human capital, thereby increasing their productive potential and making them more attractive to employers in the labor market. There are many shortcomings with this approach, and this chapter presents an alternative framework for understanding VET that brings together concepts from (neo)institutional theories, in particular the skills ecosystem approach, and the capabilities approach. With clearer insights into employer behavior and human development, this alternative framework is better able to resolve some of the critical issues in VET, such as why employers are reluctant to invest in training and how VET should be delivered and financed.
