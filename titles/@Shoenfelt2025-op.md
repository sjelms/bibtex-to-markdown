> [!abstract]
> Frederick Winslow Taylor arguably is one of the most influential management theorists of the 20th century. His Principles of Scientific Management (1911/1998) had global impact when first published and is still influential. This brief biography delineates key milestones in Taylor’s life and career including his remarkable scientific studies in the steel industry that greatly improved industrial efficiency and the economic status of workers. Taylor’s prescient insights into work performance anticipated many principles and practices foundational to industrial-organizational psychology.
