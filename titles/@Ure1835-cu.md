> [!abstract]
> Andrew Ure’s 1835 work, The Philosophy of Manufactures, offers an enthusiastic defense of the Industrial Revolution, arguing that automated machinery boosts economic efficiency, reduces costs, and replaces strenuous human labor. Ure contends that factories, defined as self-regulated "automata," transform labor by prioritizing machine-tending over skilled craftsmanship, ultimately driving global trade competitiveness.
